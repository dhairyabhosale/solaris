## Does weather add information?

`lgbm_weather` and `lgbm_calendar_history` are the same algorithm with the same hyperparameters. The only difference is that one may see the archived day-ahead temperature forecast. The gap between them is therefore the contribution of weather information.

- MAE without weather: **3,822 MW**
- MAE with weather: **3,398 MW**
- Reduction: **11.07%**
- Paired t-test on daily MAE: t = 6.50, p = 3.22e-10, n = 305 days
- Wilcoxon signed-rank p = 1.49e-10
- Mean daily MAE improvement: 432 MW (95% CI 301 to 563 MW)
- Days improved: 65.6%

## Limitations

- Only `temperature_2m` is available from Open-Meteo's Previous Runs API at a genuine day-ahead lead over 2021-2023. Humidity, radiation, cloud cover and wind forecasts are not, so they are excluded from the operational model rather than substituted with observations.
- The national weather index is population-weighted, which is a proxy for, not a measurement of, each region's share of electricity demand.
- The hot-period label is research-defined and percentile-based. It is not an India Meteorological Department heatwave declaration.
- Residual load here is metered demand minus SCADA solar and wind. It excludes behind-the-meter rooftop solar, interchange and storage, so it is an indicator of the evening ramp rather than an operator's net-load figure.
- Associations between temperature and demand reported here are predictive, not causal. No causal identification strategy is used.
