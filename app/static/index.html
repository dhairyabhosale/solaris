    🚨 Emergency Heat Triage

    If you or a coworker are experiencing severe dizziness, confusion, fainting, or have stopped sweating:
    1. STOP WORK immediately and alert a coworker or supervisor.
    2. MOVE TO SHADE or an air-conditioned area.
    3. LOOSEN CLOTHING: remove hardhat, vest, heavy gear.
    4. COOL DOWN: apply cool water or wet cloths to neck, armpits, and groin.
    5. HYDRATE: sip cool water slowly — don't chug.
    General workplace safety guidance only, not a medical diagnosis. Call emergency services immediately for unconsciousness or confusion.
